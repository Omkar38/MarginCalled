The bid-ask spread on these contracts is one cent per leg — two legs, in and out, is two cents. The edge and the cost of collecting it are the same number. This isn't the broker. Alpaca charged zero fees, and several orders filled better than we asked. It's market structure: you buy at the ask, sell at the bid, and the difference is what a market maker earns.
