Live, on a hundred thousand dollar Alpaca paper account, the agent placed a hundred and thirty one orders through the MCP server. Forty filled. Twenty one completed round trips, and every single one closed because its violation reverted. Not one closed on a time stop or a deadline. Final equity, ninety nine thousand nine hundred and sixty three dollars. A loss of thirty seven. So the mechanism worked perfectly and the account still lost money. That gap is the most useful thing this project found, and the reason is exact. The mispricing is worth about two cents. The spread on these contracts is one cent per leg. Two legs, in and out, is two cents. The edge and the cost of collecting it are the same number, so execution alone decides the sign. And this is not the broker. Alpaca charged zero fees, and orders often filled better than we asked. It is market structure.
