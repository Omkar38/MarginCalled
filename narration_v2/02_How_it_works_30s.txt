The property it exploits is total positivity of order two. For any two expiries and two strikes, the product of one pair of call prices must be at least the product of the other, once the strikes are adjusted to a common forward. Four contracts form a rectangle, and when the inequality fails, the surface is inconsistent. This is not a model. It follows from the requirement that no arbitrage exists at all, so a violation is proof the prices cannot all be right.
