The property it exploits is total positivity of order two. For any two expiries and two strikes, the product of one pair of call prices must be at least the product of the other pair, once the strikes are adjusted to a common forward. Four contracts form a rectangle. When the inequality fails, the surface is inconsistent. This is not a model. It assumes no particular dynamics — it follows from the requirement that no arbitrage exists at all. So a violation is not a signal that something is mispriced relative to a model. It is proof the prices cannot all be right.
