Most candidates never become trades. The funnel shows where they stop: the early exercise gate, the execution screens, the risk firewall. Refusing is the normal case, and the agent records its reasons as fully as its fills. Three findings stand. The signal is real and reverts on schedule. The theoretical position sizing is unusable in practice. And the binding constraint on a live account is the spread, not the mathematics. An end-of-day research paper cannot discover that, because mid prices are not tradeable and margin does not exist in a backtest.
